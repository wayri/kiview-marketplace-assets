# KiView support

Contact **support@sigharmonic.com** for KiCad Visual Review / Pocket KiView support, privacy requests or commercial licensing.

Include your browser and extension version, a description of the problem, reproduction steps, and a non-confidential minimal sample if useful. Do not send passwords, access tokens or confidential designs. Sending an email shares the information you include with the support recipient and the email services used to deliver it.

For provider-side content or account permissions, also use GitHub/GitLab's own removal and authorization controls. Deleting an extension draft does not remove a posted remote comment or attachment.
